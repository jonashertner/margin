ONE WORKSPACE, TWO WAYS IN

Stay close
to the words.

Write in Word. Review beside it. Open a local snapshot in Margin when you want more room to think.

Inside Word

Move through tracked changes, see who changed what, and accept or reject one change at a time. Your choices affect the document open in Word. Word’s Undo lets you reconsider.

In your browser

Compare versions, leave comments, keep questions for the next reader, and export Word, LaTeX or a typeset PDF. A snapshot is a separate copy; edits do not sync back to Word.

Set up on your Mac

In Margin’s project folder, install dependencies and prepare the local connection:

npm ci
npm run word:setup -- install-certificate
npm run word:setup -- sideload
npm run word:start

The certificate step may ask for your Mac’s permission. If a trusted Office development certificate is already installed, you can skip it. Then, in Word, choose Home → Add-ins → Margin. Depending on your Word version, use the Add-ins menu or its arrow to find the sideloaded add-in. Once loaded, Margin appears on the Home ribbon.

After setup, Start Margin in Word.command starts both the pane and web app. Keep its window open while you work. Read the setup guide for requirements and other hosts.

A clear boundary

Native review covers tracked changes in Word’s main text. Word remains the place to check headers, footnotes and other document areas. The browser conversion record shows what needs a closer look.

Document processing stays on your computer. The Word pane loads Microsoft’s Office runtime online. The standalone web app needs no cloud service. Word and PDF conversion currently require macOS.
